Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 July 2026

Tina helps you track what you have at home. This policy explains what we store and why.

What we store

  • Your email address, used to sign you in and to show other members of your household who you are.
  • The households, spaces and items you create, including quantities and the low-stock thresholds you set.
  • Invite codes you generate, until they expire or you revoke them.

We do not collect analytics from third parties, we do not track you across other apps, and we never sell your data.

Who can see your data

Only members of a household can read or change that household's spaces and items. This is enforced by the database itself, not just by the app.

Where it lives

Your data is stored by Supabase, our backend provider, and travels over an encrypted connection. Your login session is kept in the iOS Keychain on your device.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from Settings. When you do, we remove your account and your memberships. Households where you were the last member are deleted along with everything in them; households with other members carry on without you, and ownership passes to the longest-standing member.

Your rights

If you are in the EU or UK, the GDPR gives you the right to access, correct, export or erase your data. Account deletion in the app covers erasure. For anything else, contact us.
